## In brief

A grammar reconstructs three shield-back chair designs attributed to Samuel McIntyre and generates new variations on the same Hepplewhite paradigm.

Evidence: [pp. 227–229: Introduction and analysis of chair-back designs](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.theGenerationOfHepplewhite-styleChair-backDesigns.pdf).

## Question

How can a compact rule system capture recognizable stylistic constraints while allowing variation within them?

Evidence: [pp. 227–229: Introduction and analysis of chair-back designs](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.theGenerationOfHepplewhite-styleChair-backDesigns.pdf).

## Premise and claim

The shared structure is a compositional paradigm. Creativity can elaborate that paradigm rather than invent a completely new style.

Evidence: [pp. 227–229: Introduction and analysis of chair-back designs](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.theGenerationOfHepplewhite-styleChair-backDesigns.pdf).

## How it works

Simplify curved backs into straight-line patterns, generate one symmetric half with parameterized rules and labels, reflect it, then replace lines with curves using a separate procedure.

Evidence: [pp. 230–237: grammar, curve procedure and Figures 7, 12, 21–23](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.theGenerationOfHepplewhite-styleChair-backDesigns.pdf).

## What it establishes

The same grammar produces both reconstructions and additional designs under explicit constraints. Knight compares its compactness and usefulness to a pattern book.

Evidence: [pp. 230–237: grammar, curve procedure and Figures 7, 12, 21–23](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.theGenerationOfHepplewhite-styleChair-backDesigns.pdf); [p. 238: Conclusion](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.theGenerationOfHepplewhite-styleChair-backDesigns.pdf).

## Limits

Only one shield-back paradigm is modeled. Seats, legs and carved ornament are excluded; this is a constructive demonstration, not a historical completeness or manufacturing claim.

Evidence: [pp. 227–229: Introduction and analysis of chair-back designs](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.theGenerationOfHepplewhite-styleChair-backDesigns.pdf); [pp. 230–237: grammar, curve procedure and Figures 7, 12, 21–23](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.theGenerationOfHepplewhite-styleChair-backDesigns.pdf).
