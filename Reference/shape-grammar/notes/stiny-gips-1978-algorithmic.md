## What can be established

A 1978 Stiny–Gips book on computer models for criticism and design in the arts, published by University of California Press. [1](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/4-540-introduction-to-shape-grammars-i-fall-2018/pages/lecture-slides-and-supplemental-readings/)

## What the later author account says

Stiny recalls constructive and evocative descriptions and an evaluation measure comparing unity and variety, or order and complexity. He associates the earlier approach with algorithmic information theory and counting units in a predefined vocabulary. [2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/4-540-introduction-to-shape-grammars-i-fall-2018/87aee62d973cca8346b2f105a6d97bf6_MIT4_540F18_qa3.pdf)

## Why the retrospective matters

The later Stiny criticizes those fixed-unit commitments and contrasts them with embedding and fusion. This records an evolution in his position; it is not a direct verification or neutral substitute for the 1978 argument. [2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/4-540-introduction-to-shape-grammars-i-fall-2018/87aee62d973cca8346b2f105a6d97bf6_MIT4_540F18_qa3.pdf)

## What still needs the original

The book’s precise algorithms, assumptions, demonstrations and conclusions were not read. No general claim that it objectively measures beauty or validates automated criticism is justified here. ## Questions for reading the book

Identify how objects are described, what an evaluation measure rewards, and which examples test that measure. Compare those choices with Stiny’s later criticism while keeping the two publication contexts separate. [2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/4-540-introduction-to-shape-grammars-i-fall-2018/87aee62d973cca8346b2f105a6d97bf6_MIT4_540F18_qa3.pdf)

## Key terms

- **Constructive description**: A description concerned with how something is generated.
- **Evaluation measure**: A specified criterion used to compare interpretations or designs.
