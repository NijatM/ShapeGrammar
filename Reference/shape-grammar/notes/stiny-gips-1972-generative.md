## What it is about

Stiny and Gips specify families of geometric artworks through shape-generating rules and separate instructions for material realization.

[Reprint pp. 125–134](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/221329330_%27Shape_Grammars_and_the_Generative_Specification_of_Painting_and_Sculpture%27)

## Premise

A generative specification can describe a class independently of any one finished artwork.

[Reprint pp. 125–134](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/221329330_%27Shape_Grammars_and_the_Generative_Specification_of_Painting_and_Sculpture%27)

## The formal machinery

The reprint defines a grammar with terminals, markers, rules and an initial shape, then adds selection rules, painting instructions and a limiting canvas shape.

[Reprint pp. 125–134](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/221329330_%27Shape_Grammars_and_the_Generative_Specification_of_Painting_and_Sculpture%27)

## The demonstration

The Urform example links recursive shape generation with colouring based on level-defined regions. Sculpture extends the arrangement to three dimensions.

[Reprint pp. 125–134](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/221329330_%27Shape_Grammars_and_the_Generative_Specification_of_Painting_and_Sculpture%27)

## Proposed conclusion

Artists can choose relationships and determine their consequences algorithmically. The aesthetic discussion proposes relating compact specifications to visual complexity.

[Reprint pp. 125–134](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/221329330_%27Shape_Grammars_and_the_Generative_Specification_of_Painting_and_Sculpture%27)

## Important historical boundary

This early definition restricts terminal erasure. It should not be substituted unchanged for later, more general shape arithmetic. No controlled test of artistic quality is reported.

[Reprint pp. 125–134](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/221329330_%27Shape_Grammars_and_the_Generative_Specification_of_Painting_and_Sculpture%27)
