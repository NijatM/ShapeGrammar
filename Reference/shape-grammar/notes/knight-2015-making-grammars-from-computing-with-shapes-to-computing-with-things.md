## Short overview

Knight and Stiny propose making grammars as a computational theory of making. They extend shape grammar’s relationship between perception and action to activities involving material things.

[Repository abstract; author-final-manuscript version statement](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/7a52ee9e-621c-4e1b-b11f-22a7c73e6622) · [Abstract and highlights](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0142694X15000605)

## Premise and claim

Can the principles supporting visual shape computation describe doing and sensing with materials? The proposal starts from the improvisational features of shape grammars rather than equating making with automated fabrication.

[Repository abstract; author-final-manuscript version statement](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/7a52ee9e-621c-4e1b-b11f-22a7c73e6622)

## Method reported

The authors modify algebras for shape elements into algebras for materials, then adapt grammars from computing shapes to computing things. The abstract says examples illustrate both the grammars and their supporting algebras.

[Repository abstract; author-final-manuscript version statement](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/7a52ee9e-621c-4e1b-b11f-22a7c73e6622) · [Abstract and highlights](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0142694X15000605)

## Reported contribution

The contribution is a proposed formal framework and a reframing of designing and making. The abstract reports no controlled experiment or general fabrication-performance result.

[Repository abstract; author-final-manuscript version statement](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/7a52ee9e-621c-4e1b-b11f-22a7c73e6622) · [Abstract and highlights](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0142694X15000605)

## What needs the full paper

The actual definitions and example computations remain unchecked here. An algebra tailored to an activity should not be assumed to model every physical behaviour of its material.

[Repository abstract; author-final-manuscript version statement](https://dspace.mit.edu/entities/publication/7a52ee9e-621c-4e1b-b11f-22a7c73e6622)

## What to ask while reading

Identify the material, the actions, the sensory decisions and the operations the algebra permits. Separate a useful formal description from a physically validated simulation.
