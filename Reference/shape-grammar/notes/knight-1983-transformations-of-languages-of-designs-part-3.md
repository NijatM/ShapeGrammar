## Question

Can their apparent formal differences be explained through explicit changes to a generative organization?

Evidence: [pp. 155–156: From butterflies to polliwogs](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.transformationOfLanguagesOfDesignsPart3.pdf).

## Main claim

Basic Usonian compositions can be interpreted as transformations of the Prairie grammar, while maintaining related rule dependencies.

Evidence: [pp. 155–156: From butterflies to polliwogs](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.transformationOfLanguagesOfDesignsPart3.pdf).

## Method

Rewrite a modified subset of Koning–Eizenberg’s Prairie grammar in normal form. Change the fireplace into an interior kitchen/fireplace workspace, rearrange zones and make extensions optional.

Evidence: [pp. 155–156: From butterflies to polliwogs](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.transformationOfLanguagesOfDesignsPart3.pdf); [pp. 162–164: Spatial relations and state labels](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.transformationOfLanguagesOfDesignsPart3.pdf).

## Examples

A Garrison-house derivation and plans for Jacobs, Lusk, Newman, Rosenbaum and Pope illustrate compositions elaborated with ornamentation rules.

Evidence: [pp. 175–177: Figures 11–12 and Conclusions](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.transformationOfLanguagesOfDesignsPart3.pdf).

## Conclusion

A block-based interpretation connects the two languages explicitly. Knight argues that linked grammars can strengthen an analysis of style while allowing alternative interpretations.

Evidence: [pp. 175–177: Figures 11–12 and Conclusions](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.transformationOfLanguagesOfDesignsPart3.pdf).

## Limits

The normal-form Prairie grammar omits some original rules and admits some additional possibilities. A formal connection is an interpretation, not proof that Wright followed this exact procedure.

Evidence: [pp. 155–156: From butterflies to polliwogs](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.transformationOfLanguagesOfDesignsPart3.pdf); [pp. 175–177: Figures 11–12 and Conclusions](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.transformationOfLanguagesOfDesignsPart3.pdf).

## Why read it

Changing control can matter as much as changing shape: obligatory versus optional extensions changes the organization of possible plans.

Evidence: [pp. 162–164: Spatial relations and state labels](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Knight.transformationOfLanguagesOfDesignsPart3.pdf).
