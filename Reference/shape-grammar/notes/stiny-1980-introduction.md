# Introduction to Shape and Shape Grammars

George Stiny · 1980

Review basis: selected-sections. Primary paper: shape operations and labels, formal grammar definition, square derivation and parametric extension.

The main definitions and worked examples were reviewed; no independent verification of every formal detail is implied. This uses the public teaching copy, not the unrelated local-only PDF.

## In brief

A compact reference for what a shape grammar formally contains and how a rule acts. The paper connects geometric parts, labels, recursive derivation and parametric rule families. ## The question

How can an algorithm be stated directly with drawings while precisely specifying matching, replacement and which outputs belong to its language? [1](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf) [2](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf)

## Main idea or claim

A rule recognizes an embedded geometric part under a transformation and replaces it using the same transformation. Labels can direct this process without becoming permanent features of the final drawing. [2](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf)

## How the work approaches it

Stiny defines shapes and operations, adds labelled points, specifies a grammar’s components, and derives a nested-square example. The parametric extension assigns coordinates under constraints to obtain a particular rule from a schema. [1](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf) [2](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf) [3](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf)

## Evidence and examples

In the square example, a marker identifies the square to elaborate. One rule inscribes another square and moves the marker; another removes the marker to produce an unlabelled language member. [2](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf)

## What comes out of it

The formula C′ = (C − t(A)) + t(B) unifies erasing and adding. Parametric grammars extend variation beyond a shape’s location, orientation and scale to permitted changes in its proportions or geometry. [2](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf) [3](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf)

## Limits to keep in mind

This is a definitional introduction, not an efficiency or usability study. Its general transformation possibilities exceed this app’s finite search menu. Parameter assignment and geometric placement are distinct operations. [2](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf) [3](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf)

## What to take from it

Track three things separately: the pattern being recognized, its transformation, and the rule’s replacement. Ask whether a visible label is a construction control or a desired final feature. [2](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf) [3](https://www.andrew.cmu.edu/user/ramesh/teaching/course/48-747/subFrames/readings/Stiny-1980-EPB7_343-351.IntroductionToShapeAndShapeGrammars.pdf)
