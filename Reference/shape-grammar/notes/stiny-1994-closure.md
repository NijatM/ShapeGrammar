## Short overview

Stiny connects shape-grammar computation with topologies constructed through closure operations.

[Public abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1068/b21S049) · [Publisher-deposited abstract](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1068/b21S049)

## The stated claim

The abstract says these topologies structure shapes according to their computations, allowing emergence to coexist with continuity.

[Public abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1068/b21S049) · [Publisher-deposited abstract](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1068/b21S049)

## What remains unchecked

The short abstract provides neither proofs nor worked examples. Continuity here should not be assumed to mean smooth visual motion or a prohibition on new parts.

[Public abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1068/b21S049) · [Publisher-deposited abstract](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1068/b21S049)

## Reading question

Ask which topology is assigned, how the computation determines it and which map is proved continuous. Those are questions for the original, not reconstructed results.
