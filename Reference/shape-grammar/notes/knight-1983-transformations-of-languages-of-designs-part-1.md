## Question or aim

How can stylistic relationships and innovation be stated through transformations?

Evidence: [Abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1068/b100125).

Evidence: [Abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1068/b100125).
