## What it is about

Knight uses Kandinsky and Klee’s writings as a framework for considering divisions in computational design. Their resistance to rigid oppositions in art provides the starting point.

[Public abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1068/b12927)

## Question and premise

Which apparent either/or choices are helpful, and which obscure how design works? The essay asks whether divisions inherited from debates about abstraction can be reconciled in computational design.

[Public abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1068/b12927)

## Method of argument

The public abstract describes a conceptual comparison between twentieth-century art theory and contemporary computational-design debates. It does not describe an experiment.

[Public abstract](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1068/b12927)

## What can be said reliably

The essay considers reconciliation rather than simply accepting mutually exclusive categories. The abstract does not identify every opposing pair or provide a checked final resolution.

## A question to carry forward

When choosing a design method, ask whether two approaches must exclude one another or could play complementary roles. That is a reading prompt, not a tested finding of this guide.
