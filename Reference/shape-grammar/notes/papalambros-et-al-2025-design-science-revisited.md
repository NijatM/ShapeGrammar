## Retrospective question

What has the journal and its community accomplished, and what challenges should design research address next?

## Knight’s position

Knight again interprets design science as studying design. She defends the field’s own history and diverse methods, including systematic analysis and improvisational practice, while emphasizing social, intellectual, bodily and perceptual human capacities.

Evidence: [pp. 26–27: Terry Knight](https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/94756/1/Papalambros-etal-DS-2025-Design-science-why-what-and-how-revisited.pdf).

## How the contribution was assembled

Editors were invited to reflect independently, with constraints on length and references. The authors explicitly declined to harmonize or synthesize the statements, unlike the 2015 editorial.

Evidence: [pp. 2–4: Abstract, Introduction and opening Editors-in-Chief reflections](https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/94756/1/Papalambros-etal-DS-2025-Design-science-why-what-and-how-revisited.pdf).

## Knight’s examples

She points to an expanded range of design-related disciplines at MIT and describes teaching generative work through shape and making grammars using hand, eye and touch.

Evidence: [pp. 26–27: Terry Knight](https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/94756/1/Papalambros-etal-DS-2025-Design-science-why-what-and-how-revisited.pdf).

## Her concluding direction

Knight argues that studying these human forms of designing can contribute to broader efforts in design research. Her remarks about automation are a reflective position and teaching observation, not a benchmark comparing people with AI.

Evidence: [pp. 26–27: Terry Knight](https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/94756/1/Papalambros-etal-DS-2025-Design-science-why-what-and-how-revisited.pdf).

## Limits

There is no single experimental method or consensus conclusion across this editorial. Claims from one contributor should not be attributed to every author. The opening retrospective does not substitute for a systematic evaluation of the field.

Evidence: [pp. 2–4: Abstract, Introduction and opening Editors-in-Chief reflections](https://strathprints.strath.ac.uk/94756/1/Papalambros-etal-DS-2025-Design-science-why-what-and-how-revisited.pdf).

## Why read it

Compare Knight’s 2015 and 2025 statements: the commitment to broad, rigorous design study continues, with renewed attention to what human generative activity involves.
