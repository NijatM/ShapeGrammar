## What can be established here

This is Conway and Norton’s 1979 paper in Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society, volume 11, pages 308–339. [1](https://londmathsoc.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1112/blms/11.3.308)

## Claim described by the later proof

Borcherds explains their central conjecture as the existence of a graded Monster module whose element-trace series are specified genus-zero Hauptmoduls. His theorem establishes this for the Frenkel–Lepowsky–Meurman module. [2](https://math.berkeley.edu/~reb/papers/monster/monster.pdf)

## Key terms

- **Graded module**: A representation split into indexed vector-space pieces.
- **Hauptmodul**: A generator of a genus-zero modular function field.
