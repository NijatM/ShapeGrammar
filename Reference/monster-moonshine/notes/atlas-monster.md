## How the reference supports computation

Black-box recipes seek elements in suitable classes, conjugate them and test product orders. Additional checks distinguish acceptable standard generators; the opening order conditions are not a freestanding construction of the Monster. [2](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M/)

## Reading the evidence correctly

This is a data-and-algorithm resource rather than a paper reporting an experiment. Several representation notes are dated 1997–2000. Treat their machine timings and availability statements historically. [2](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M/)

## Best use here

Use the entry to verify exact group size and notation before reading moonshine surveys. Do not infer a complete modern subgroup classification from this page alone. [1](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M/) [2](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M/)

## Key terms

- **Order**: Number of elements in a finite group, or least positive identity power of an element.
- **Conjugacy class**: Elements related by changing coordinates through conjugation.
