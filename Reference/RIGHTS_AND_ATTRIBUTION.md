## Deliberately linked rather than copied

- The existing Stiny 1980 PDF: exact-file provenance and redistribution permission not established; preserved locally, excluded from proposed staging.
- Stiny’s 2018 lecture slides: PDF pages 4, 38 and 44 explicitly exclude third-party content from the CC license. The course-wide license does not authorize mirroring the complete slide deck.
- Haridis–Stiny’s continuity preprint: the verified [arXiv license](https://arxiv.org/licenses/nonexclusive-distrib/1.0/license.html) grants distribution rights to arXiv, not a general mirroring license to this project.
- Author-hosted Borcherds papers, journal articles and ATLAS data: lawful reading links are retained; no general redistribution license was established here.
- *Shapes of Imagination*: publisher identifies CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and a lawful OA DOI, but a complete download was not retrieved. Its record preserves the link and rights evidence.
- Milne v4.01: web PDF was readable, direct download returned HTTP 406, and its license paragraph explicitly names v4.0. Link retained; no local file claimed.

No private browser notes, progress records or experiments were collected. Editorial notes in this folder are intended to be public. Do not copy this complete directory indiscriminately into a future website build: select bibliography data and approved assets deliberately.
