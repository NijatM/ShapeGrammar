# Connections and prerequisites

This folder identifies concrete preparation routes. It does not assert an established chain from Stiny’s theory to the Monster. [Catalogue](../CATALOGUE.md#bridges).

| Starting point | Next subject | Concrete question | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shape rule transformations | Sets and functions | What is the domain of a transformation, and how is composition ordered? | Hammack Chapter 12; Judson §1.2 |
| Square symmetry | Group actions | What is a symmetry transformation, what does it act on, and what fixes a chosen vertex? | Judson §§5.2 and 14.1 |
| Proposed finite match menu | Closure and groups | Why can two individually allowed matches compose to an unsupported transform? | Group theory topic note; Judson §3.2 |
| Plane symmetry | Crystallographic symmetry | How do repetition and symmetry operations structure a pattern? | [IUCr, Symmetry](https://www.iucr.org/what-we-do/education/pamphlets/symmetry) |
| Finite groups | Linear representations | How can a group act by invertible linear maps? | [Hefferon](https://hefferon.net/linearalgebra/); [Etingof et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.0827v5) |
| Representations | Moonshine | What additional structure relates dimensions and modular functions? | Borcherds surveys; Gannon |

Hefferon provides preparation in vector spaces, linear maps, matrices and eigenvalues. Etingof et al. explicitly expect strong linear algebra and basic abstract algebra. IUCr’s introductory symmetry treatment is institutional teaching material; it is not a complete first course in frieze or wallpaper groups.

Later research should proceed question by question: define a shape family and transformation action, preserve the experiment’s exact domain, distinguish empirical invariance from proof, find counterexamples, then identify relevant mathematics. Do not infer a group solely from visual complexity or a finite menu of transformations.
