## Two distinctions worth retaining

An action is faithful when no nonidentity element fixes everything. It is free when every point stabilizer is trivial. These are different requirements. A representation is an action by invertible linear maps. [1](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/GT.pdf) [2](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/GT.pdf)

## How the proof works

Maschke’s theorem constructs an invariant complement to an invariant subspace by averaging a projector over the group. Over real or complex spaces, averaging an appropriate positive-definite form provides another approach. [2](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/GT.pdf)

## Conclusion and hypothesis

For finite G, if the field characteristic does not divide |G|, every invariant subspace has an invariant complement. The division by |G| in the averaging formula explains why that field condition matters. [2](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/GT.pdf)

## Why this is a useful bridge

An action gives a homomorphism into permutations; requiring linear maps gives a homomorphism into GL(V). This is the transition from visual symmetry to the representation language used in moonshine. [1](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/GT.pdf) [2](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/GT.pdf)

## Key terms

- **Faithful**: Distinct group elements induce distinct transformations.
- **Invariant subspace**: A subspace preserved by every group element.
- **Projector**: A linear map P satisfying P²=P.
