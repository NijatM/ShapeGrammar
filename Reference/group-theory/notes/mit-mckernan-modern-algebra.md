## What these notes help connect

A short route from triangle motions to abstract groups, cosets, quotients and actions. The central theme is turning an intuitive classification into a structure whose operations are actually well-defined. ## The central checks

An equivalence relation partitions a set. Cosets give such a partition, but multiplying classes requires an extra check: choosing different representatives must give the same class. Normality makes the proposed quotient multiplication work. [2](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-703-modern-algebra-spring-2013/df580757da0149c376f1fda29bf82a91_MIT18_703S13_pra_l_3.pdf) [3](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-703-modern-algebra-spring-2013/ecdbd211f736b3ac92379dea05b34491_MIT18_703S13_pra_l_9.pdf)

## Method of explanation

The notes use an intentionally bad example: assigning a height to an eye-color class by selecting a person. Different representatives give different heights. The same danger motivates the algebraic proof for quotient groups. [3](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-703-modern-algebra-spring-2013/ecdbd211f736b3ac92379dea05b34491_MIT18_703S13_pra_l_9.pdf)

## From classes to actions

A transitive action identifies its acted-on set with the cosets of one stabilizer. For a finite group, orbit size equals group order divided by stabilizer order. The correspondence explains the count rather than merely fitting examples. [4](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-703-modern-algebra-spring-2013/96221ce2d5b1077b3e5d3c5e08c88afe_MIT18_703S13_pra_l_23.pdf)

## A practical reading habit

Whenever an operation uses a representative, check independence from that choice. Whenever two transformations seem identical, specify what set they act on. Read compositions rightmost-first in these notes. [1](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-703-modern-algebra-spring-2013/faeeeaf01f838584ce2172cfd85cf74d_MIT18_703S13_pra_l_1.pdf) [3](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-703-modern-algebra-spring-2013/ecdbd211f736b3ac92379dea05b34491_MIT18_703S13_pra_l_9.pdf) [4](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-703-modern-algebra-spring-2013/96221ce2d5b1077b3e5d3c5e08c88afe_MIT18_703S13_pra_l_23.pdf)

## Key terms

- **Well-defined**: Independent of arbitrary choices used in a definition.
- **Normal subgroup**: A subgroup preserved by every conjugation.
- **Stabilizer**: The subgroup fixing a chosen object.
