# Terry Knight — works and teaching materials

Research checked on 18 October 2026. **55 canonical records involving Knight**, from 1980 through 2026: authored/coauthored works, a named contribution, edited-issue contexts and teaching materials. Five associated open-license PDFs are saved unmodified. These counts are not counts of distinct research papers.

The aim is comprehensive coverage of publicly identifiable work alongside the [George Stiny index](../george-stiny/README.md). This is a broad reconciled bibliography, not a certified complete lifetime bibliography: no complete current author-supplied CV was available in the checked sources. Some versions and older reports remain unresolved; see the [coverage audit](COVERAGE_AUDIT.md).

Canonical metadata and originals remain in their [shape grammar topic folders](../../shape-grammar/README.md), with cross-topic tags for symmetry, color, education and making. This author index avoids duplicating files.

## Reading paths

- **Design languages and change:** Languages of Designs; the three 1983 transformation papers; the 1986 meander study; Transformations in Design (1994).
- **Designing grammars:** Designing with Grammars; Designing a Shape Grammar; Six Types; Five Questions; Slow Computing.
- **Emergence and ambiguity:** Classical and Non-classical Computation with Stiny; Computing with Emergence; Computing with Ambiguity; Either/or → and.
- **Color and symmetry:** Color Grammars (1989, 1993); Mughul Gardens Revisited; Shape Grammars and Color Grammars in Design; Constructive Symmetry; Infinite Patterns and Their Symmetries.
- **Materials and making:** Visual-physical Design Grammars; Making Grammars with Stiny; Shapes and Other Things; Craft, Performance, and Grammars; later drawing, robotics and material-informed work.
- **Teaching:** MIT OCW Inquiry into Computation and Design (2006), Slow Computing (2012) and the Visual Computing syllabus (2025). Also consult the [official group course index](https://computationalmaking.mit.edu/courses/). Course supervision does not imply authorship of student work.

The group site sometimes returned 502; cached institutional entries and DOI records supplied corroboration. ORCID listed only three works in this pass and cannot stand in for the whole corpus.

Works by unrelated same-name researchers, acknowledgments, student theses supervised by Knight and papers written only by members of her group are not attributed to her.
