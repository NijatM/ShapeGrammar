## Exclusions and scope

The same name occurs in medicine, employment research, agriculture and music. Those works were excluded where identity did not match the MIT design researcher. *Creative Brickwork* under Terry L. Knight was held out because identity with Terry Weissman Knight was not established. Acknowledged advice, thesis supervision and group membership alone do not establish authorship.

No complete current CV, complete dissertation access or comprehensive archive of unpublished talks was available in the checked sources. The unresolved items above are visible research leads. Thus “all works” is the collection goal; the present deliverable is the broad verified bibliography plus an explicit reconciliation log, not a guaranteed exhaustive bibliography or an archive of every full text.
