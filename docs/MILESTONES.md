| Area | Delivered |
| --- | --- |
| Exploration | 34 study notes across three tracks, worked examples, 68 quick checks, practice/hints/solutions and track navigation |

| Distribution | Curated static build, local path/private-file checks, manually triggered Pages workflow prepared for the owner |

The [release report](RELEASE_VALIDATION.md) records tests and practical limits. The [experiment report](EXPERIMENTS.md) distinguishes the two small weight/quality-overlap models from complete color, material or weight grammar interpreters. Advanced general algebras, unrestricted continuous matching, multiuser synchronization and full scholarly proof review remain beyond this release's stated scope. Nothing has been published by the local build.
