# Source reading review

Initial review 19 October 2026; reading recovery updated 20 October 2026. There is one reading-guide file for each of the 98 canonical source records. Guides are original, condensed explanations tied to the text actually available, not reproduced abstracts. No new original publications were mirrored for this review.

## Coverage and meaning

| Review basis | Records | What the label establishes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Full text reviewed | 20 | The text was read in full; exercises, experiments and proofs were not independently reproduced. |
| Selected sections reviewed | 43 | The named sections or excerpts were read. This does not mean the whole book or paper was reviewed. |
| Abstract / publisher description | 22 | Claims are limited to an available abstract, chapter abstract or publisher description. Detailed methods and results remain unverified. |
| Publication details only | 10 | Publication identity and available context, without an account of an unread argument. |
| Supporting citation only | 3 | An explicit later citation or author retrospective; the original work was not available for direct review. |

The 63 full-text/selected-section records include research papers, editorials, books, teaching material and software documentation. They are not 63 papers read cover to cover. Selected-section labels also cover limited indexed primary excerpts where direct access failed; those restrictions are stated on the page. Abstract-based and citation-only records deliberately do not invent experiments, sample sizes, results or conclusions.

## Reading experience

Source pages put the substantive notes first, with an overview, the question or premise, approach, evidence/examples, findings, limits and takeaways where the available text supports them. Section buttons move within the page. Numbered citations open the passage evidence. Reading routes provide original URLs and page/section locators; publication details and rights remain available below. Useful terms are defined where they help.

Books are summarized only at the reviewed chapter/section level. Edited issues are treated as collections, and named contributors retain their actual role. A proposal or essay is not relabelled as an empirical study. Algorithms and mathematical arguments are described with their assumptions, without claiming independent formal verification. Temporary reading/OCR copies stayed outside the project; the 20 existing public originals retain their bytes and hashes.

## Specific qualifications

- Krishnamurti 1980: ordered same-carrier arithmetic bounds are not a bound for unrestricted matching. The 1981 companion distinguishes finite matching from representative conventions for infinite families.
- Yue–Krishnamurti: locators use the author prepublication draft. NP membership alone does not imply hardness; the discussion does not prove P ≠ NP.
- Stiny 1972: the inspected author-uploaded reprint has different pagination from the cited proceedings.
- Stiny 2018: course-hosted manuscript excerpts are distinguished from the final 2022 book. The later books remain limited to accessible publisher material where their interiors could not be retrieved.
- Design Science 2015 credits Knight as a named contributor; the 2025 reflections are not presented as one consensual experimental conclusion.
- The Etingof PDF has conflicting date labels, and Gannon’s printed Monster-order power differs from the authoritative order. Both qualifications are recorded in their guides.
- Mathematics for Computer Science’s inspected 2015 PDF carries CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; it remains a reading link, not a newly mirrored original.
