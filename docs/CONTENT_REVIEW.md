# Content review and accuracy boundaries

Reviewed 19 October 2026. This release contains 34 original introductory micro-lessons, 68 answer-key checks, worked examples and practice solutions, plus 10 research pathways and 33 glossary entries. It is an introductory study resource, not a replacement for the cited books or an independently peer-reviewed course.

## Definitions and conventions

The shape course follows Stiny 1980 printed pp. 343–344 and 347 for maximal lines, non-strict parthood, dimensional arithmetic and the shared transformation in rule application. Printed pp. 344 and 347 were visually inspected. Stouffs 2016 p. 331 supplies the Uᵢⱼ dimensional convention. The [model contract](MATHEMATICAL_MODEL.md) states the implementation's rational-coordinate and finite-menu restrictions.

The lessons consistently distinguish:

- A geometric part from an element of an original stroke list.
- Non-strict parthood from strict containment, set membership and set inclusion.
- Closed residual line-shape difference from literal point-set subtraction.
- One-dimensional common intervals from point-only crossings.
- Rule-side transformation from independently placing the replacement.
- Transformation witnesses, distinct matched regions and distinct results.
- Exhausting one finite search domain from unrestricted matching or derivation termination.

Set lessons use Hammack Chapters 1, 11, 12 and 14 for their reading locators. Worked examples were reviewed for membership versus inclusion, image versus codomain, preimage versus inverse function, composition order, equivalence classes and finite/infinite counting. The token explorer treats input tokens as literal strings; it is not a parser for nested mathematical sets.

Group lessons use Judson's introductory groups, permutations, dihedral groups, homomorphisms, normal/quotient groups and actions chapters. D₄ denotes the square symmetry group of order eight. Products apply the rightmost operation first; `f90=RF`, where F reflects across the horizontal axis. The explorer's complete multiplication table, all 512 associativity triples, inverses, and four orbit/stabilizer examples are computed and tested.

## Examples and assessments

All 34 worked examples and 68 correct options/explanations were read for internal consistency. The geometry examples cover fused intervals, gaps, diagonal carriers, one shared transform, identity rules, recursive extension and witness ambiguity. Finite-set operations, half-unit geometric probes and transformation counts have executable checks. Practice answers that admit multiple designs are presented as examples or criteria, not unique solutions. Practice confirmation is self-reported; completion is not a qualification.

No historical diagram was copied into an exercise. Architectural examples are original, narrowly scoped teaching prompts; the site does not claim to reconstruct complete Palladian or Knight precedent grammars. Color, material and making theories are reading topics rather than advertised engine features.

The Monster pathway checks its exact order against ATLAS and Borcherds's introductory article. An integer test independently multiplies the stated prime powers. The arithmetic 196884=1+196883 is an introductory numerical observation, not a proof of moonshine. The proof and historical-problems pathways do not assert current open-problem status or certify the primary proofs. See [ATLAS](https://brauer.maths.qmul.ac.uk/Atlas/spor/M/) and [Borcherds's introduction](https://math.berkeley.edu/~reb/papers/whatismonster/whatismonster.pdf).

## Source review scope

Each canonical record retains its own evidence level: bibliography, front matter/contents, selected passage, or more detailed reading. Adding an authored lesson does not upgrade every related paper to a full-text mathematical review. [Coverage](../Reference/COVERAGE.md) preserves unresolved original-paper locators. The Knight collection contains 55 associated records and an explicit [coverage audit](../Reference/authors/terry-knight/COVERAGE_AUDIT.md); it is not claimed as an exhaustive collected works.

All 20 mirrored originals retain their bytes, checksums, notices and per-file license conditions. Unlicensed-to-mirror papers use reading links. The local pre-existing Stiny PDF stays excluded from the public build. HTTP reachability is checked separately from content accuracy and redistribution rights.
